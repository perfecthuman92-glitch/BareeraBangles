PAYMENT METHODS
---------------
Provide payment options:
1. JazzCash
2. Bank Card
IMPORTANT:
Do not implement fake payment processing.
Structure the application so that a real payment gateway can be integrated securely.
For JazzCash:
- Create a proper payment-service abstraction/interface.
- Keep credentials/configuration in appsettings/user secrets/environment variables.
- Do not hardcode merchant credentials.
- Provide a clean integration point for JazzCash API/payment gateway.
For Bank Card:
- Create a payment service abstraction suitable for integrating a real card-payment provider such as Stripe or another supported gateway.
- Never store raw card numbers, CVV, or sensitive card information in the database.
- Use the payment provider's hosted/secure checkout mechanism.
The checkout should allow:
Payment Method:
( ) JazzCash
( ) Bank Card
